# 🔒 Case 001.2 — Solution (spoilers)

**Who:** Dr Helen Park.

**Weapon:** the gilt picture frame of *Evening, Waitematā*. Square edge and two parallel pin marks in the wound (Stop 0). Tessa's pin spacing (Stop 3). Gilt splinters at the fountain (Stop 4). A snap heard in the university (Stop 7).

**Why:** Two years ago Dr Park authenticated the painting as a genuine 1893 Eliza Crane and took a fee from the seller. Julian's conservator found titanium white (Stop 3), proving a 20th-century forgery. Julian called her department at 11:42pm (Stop 4) and asked her to meet in the park. He planned to cancel the sale and tell Marcus the truth, which would end her career.

**How:** They argued by the fountain. She took hold of the frame and painting; he held on; she struck him once with the frame. Not planned. She panicked, broke the frame to remove the canvas, and walked up to the university carrying the roll (groundskeeper, Stop 4; cleaner, Stop 7).

**Where is the painting:** In her office at the university. The frame was broken and the pieces hidden. The "stolen" painting was worthless all along.
