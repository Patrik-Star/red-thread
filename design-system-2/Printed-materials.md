# Printed materials

Red Thread is played in the real world, so much of the brand is physical. These are starting guidelines for the case kit.

## The case box / envelope
- A kraft or black envelope with the case name in Instrument Serif and the case number in IBM Plex Mono (`label`).
- **A real red thread.** Thread a length of red yarn through a punched hole and wind it round a brass split pin on the flap. Teams pull the thread to open the case. The brand motif becomes the first thing they touch.
- A tamper strip in `manila` with the words "CASE FILE · DO NOT OPEN UNTIL BRIEFED".

## Game cards vs evidence (the two-layer rule in print)
The same rule as on screen: **the game is rounded, the story is square.**

- **Game cards** (briefing, rules, hint cards, the accusation sheet): `note` off-white stock (a warm uncoated, not bright white), **rounded corners** (a 4 mm corner punch), Inter and IBM Plex Mono. No shadows to fake; they're flat by nature.
- **Evidence** (receipts, tickets, letters, call records, photos): **pure white stock, square-cut corners**, printed in the document's own type. Real thermal paper for receipts if possible; it fades over time, so print a fresh batch per playtest and keep spares. Torn edges are torn by hand, not printed.
- **Evidence tags**: `manila` card with rounded corners and a punched hole, tied or clipped to each object. Number in `manila-thread`, `EVIDENCE 04` in mono, title in serif, and "Found" location under a dashed tear line. The tag is the game; the object it's tied to is the story.
- Print a short red thread segment from each tag's hole off its right edge, continuing at the next tag in sequence, so tags laid out in order form one line. The thread never crosses an object.

## Map
- A single-colour CBD street map in `ink-muted` on `ground` (Night) or `ground` (Paper). Locations are brass pins, joined by the red thread in the order the teams discover them. Print it without the thread so teams can draw their own in red pen.

## Posters & social
- Night ground, a `display-xl` headline, one photograph graded cool, and the thread entering from one edge, crossing behind the headline and exiting another.
- Margins of `space-16` (64px at 1080px wide). Keep it to one headline and one line of detail: date, meeting point and "3–6 players".

## Print colour
`thread` #E4142C is close to Pantone 185 C, a bright signal red. Uncoated stock dulls and darkens it, so proof on the real card, and consider a fluorescent-boosted red or a spot colour for posters that must glow at night. `manila` #D6C8A8 works as a stock colour, so choose real manila or kraft card over a printed flood.
